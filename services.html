Included & Not Included

What’s in a Typical Package?

Included

  • Airport pickup and drop-off
  • Accommodation (tea houses or hotels)
  • Breakfasts and some dinners (varies by package)
  • Licensed local trekking guide
  • Trekking permits and park fees

Not Included

  • International flights to/from Nepal
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal trekking gear and tips
  • Lunches and extra snacks
