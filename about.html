Who We Are

About Namaste Trek & Tours

A fictional travel agency designed as a class website project, inspired by real-world trekking companies in Nepal.

Company Background

Namaste Trek & Tours was “founded” in this project to represent a modern travel agency that specializes in personalized trekking and cultural experiences in Nepal. The company focuses on small groups, safety, and sustainable tourism.

Although this is not a real company, the structure, pages, and content are modeled after a professional travel business website, including packages, gallery, contact form, and comparison of productivity tools.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To help travelers of all backgrounds explore Nepal safely while respecting local culture, people, and the environment.

Vision: To be recognized as a responsible travel partner that connects visitors and local communities through unforgettable Himalayan experiences.

Values: Safety, respect for culture, fair wages for local guides, environmental protection, and honest communication with guests.
