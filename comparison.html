Productivity Tools

MS Office vs Google Apps vs AI Apps (Gamma)

Comparison based on advantages, disadvantages, and ease of use for creating content such as this website project.

Tool Advantages Disadvantages Ease of Use (1–5)
MS PowerPoint Works offline, rich design features, widely used in schools and businesses, strong formatting control. Requires installation and license; collaboration features are weaker unless using OneDrive/SharePoint. 4
Google Slides Free for most users, cloud-based, easy sharing and real-time collaboration, integrates with Google Drive. Fewer advanced design features; depends on internet connection; interface can feel limited for complex layouts. 4
Gamma (AI presentation tool) Quickly generates slide decks from text prompts; good for brainstorming, structure, and visual ideas. Output must be reviewed carefully; customization can be limited; requires understanding of prompts. 3.5

Ease of use scores are subjective and based on student experience while building this project. You can adjust them based on your own opinion.
